PHOTO INSTRUCTIONS FOR COMMANDER SURPRISE:
===========================================
To show your real photo to Commander:
1. Choose any photo of yourself (JPG, JPEG, or PNG).
2. Copy it into this folder: d:\estfw\assets\
3. Rename the photo to: my-photo.jpg
   (Or change the filename in shared.js under SITE_CONFIG.surprise.photoSrc).
